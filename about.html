Founded in Geneva by master horologist Lorenzo Alberto, our independent maison has spent nearly a century pursuing absolute chronometric beauty.

Maison Philosophy

INDEPENDENCE & PERMANENCE

Remaining fiercely independent allows Alberto Watch Company to reject mass production. We craft fewer than 3,800 numbered timepieces each year, ensuring every watch leaves our Geneva manufacture as a future heirloom.

Our design philosophy balances classical golden-ratio proportions with bold sculptural lugs, ensuring an Alberto timepiece looks equally timeless in 1928, 2026, and 2126.

"A watch is the only machine worn directly over the human pulse—it must beat with equal soul." — Lorenzo Alberto, 1928
Historical Milestones

CHRONICLES OF THE MAISON

1928 — Foundation in Geneva

Lorenzo Alberto opens his first horological workshop on Rue du Rhône, specializing in ultra-thin pocket chronometers for European royalty.

1957 — The Racing Chronograph

Launch of the legendary Calibre AL-V57 column-wheel chronograph, adopted by endurance drivers across Monaco and Le Mans.

1984 — First Flying Tourbillon

Alberto unveils its cantilevered tourbillon without an upper bridge, winning the Geneva Grand Prix d'Horlogerie award.

2026 — The Imperator & Chronos Era

Uniting our 18k gold Grand Complications with the aerospace titanium Chronos Connected series—bridging mechanical heritage and tomorrow's intelligence.
